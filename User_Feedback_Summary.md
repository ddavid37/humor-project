What I changed based on feedback
Merged the upload controls into one clickable area — no more confusion between the dark zone and the button. Now it says "Select an image above to get started" and then "Image selected — click Generate below."

Added a timer during generation — shows elapsed seconds and "this can take 15–30 seconds" so users know it's working.

Green flash + "Done" badge when captions arrive — so you don't miss the result. Vote buttons also show "Voted!" before moving to the next caption.
